# Product

## Summary

Monik Chatterjee's personal portfolio helps recruiters and hiring managers evaluate Monik for professional opportunities. Other visitors can explore the work and get in touch.

## Platform

Web.

## Users

- **Primary:** Recruiters and hiring managers assessing Monik's fit for opportunities.
- **Also:** Visitors interested in Monik's projects or contacting Monik.

## Core user outcome

Visitors can understand Monik's professional background and projects, then contact Monik.

## Current product

- Home and about pages present Monik and the professional profile.
- A projects section presents work backed by GitHub.
- The contact flow verifies the visitor's email address.
